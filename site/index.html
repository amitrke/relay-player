Subnext Player

A media player that plays from sources you connect yourself: a Plex server, files on your device, a folder you choose, or a network share on your own network. It hosts no content and ships no catalogue.

The app has no backend. No account, no ads, no analytics. Credentials stay in encrypted storage on your device and your viewing history never leaves it.
