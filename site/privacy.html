Privacy Policy

Subnext Player. Last updated 23 September 2026.

Subnext Player has no backend. There is no Subnext Player server, no account to create, and nowhere for us to send your data even if we wanted to. This policy is short because there is very little to describe.

What the app does

Subnext Player plays media from sources you connect yourself: a Plex server, files on your device, a folder you choose (on Android), or a network share on your own network. It hosts no content and ships no catalogue.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app remembers is stored on your device only:

  • Credentials and access tokens for the sources you connect are held in the operating system's encrypted storage.
  • Your viewing history, resume positions, favourites and settings are held in a local database on the device.

None of this is transmitted to us, because there is no us to transmit it to. Uninstalling the app removes it.

What leaves your device

Only connections to the services you have configured, made directly from your device to them:

  • Your Plex server and Plex's own sign-in service, when you link an account.
  • A network share on your local network, when you add one.
  • Any other source you choose to configure.

Those services have their own privacy policies and their own terms, and your relationship with them is yours rather than ours. We are not an intermediary, we do not proxy these connections, and we cannot see them.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics and no usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No device fingerprinting.
  • No account, because there is nothing to sign up for.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information, because none is collected.

Crash reporting

The current version of Subnext Player sends no crash reports. Optional crash reporting may be added in a future version. If it is, it will be off unless you turn it on, it will be described here before that version ships, and it will never include your credentials, your server addresses or anything about what you watch.

Children

Subnext Player is not directed at children and is not designed for use by them. It plays whatever you point it at and performs no content filtering of its own.

Permissions

The app asks for access to video on your device only so it can play it, and only reads what it is given. It never requests broad filesystem access.

  • Android: the scoped media permission for video. Folder access is granted by you, per folder, through the system picker.
  • iPhone and iPad: access to your photo library, where you can allow all of it or only the items you select. Nothing in it is changed or uploaded.
  • Both: permission to connect to devices on your local network, used only to reach a Plex server or network share you add.

Changes to this policy
